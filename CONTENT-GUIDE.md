# Learning Content Guide

Every major concept should answer eight learner questions:

1. **What is it?** A short, accurate definition.
2. **Why does it exist?** The problem it solves.
3. **How does it work?** A small sequence the learner can follow.
4. **Show me.** A visual model or diagram.
5. **Give me an example.** Prefer a concrete agent scenario.
6. **How does it fail?** Include a common mistake or failure mode.
7. **Let me try it.** Give the learner a decision, exercise, or simulation.
8. **What should I learn next?** Connect it to related concepts.

## Levels

- **Beginner:** understandable with no AI background.
- **Intermediate:** assumes the learner understands models, agents, and basic tool use.
- **Advanced:** architecture, security, evaluation, coordination, or production concerns.

## Framework policy

Frameworks are examples, not the curriculum. Explain the underlying pattern first, then show how tools such as LangGraph, AutoGen, CrewAI, Flowise, n8n, provider SDKs, or MCP implementations express that pattern.

## Accuracy policy

Avoid claims that depend on a model provider’s current pricing, limits, or product behavior unless they are dated and sourced. Core concept pages should stay useful even when individual frameworks change.
